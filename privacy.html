Privacy Policy

How RotRun processes information on your device, through Apple services on iPhone, and in your RotRun account on Android.

Introduction

RotRun is a digital-wellbeing app for iPhone and Android.

On iPhone, it helps you limit distracting apps with Apple Screen Time, run Focus sessions, score “Brain Health,” complete sit-up “Couch Reset” unlocks, and optionally log runs to Apple Health.

On Android, it helps you limit distracting apps with Usage Access and Accessibility, run Focus sessions, and score “Brain Health.” You can sign in with Google, talk to Rot in chat, and use a live camera preview called Mirror. Android does not use Apple Screen Time, Face ID, Apple Health, sit-up counting, Game Center, or StoreKit.

This policy describes what RotRun processes on your device and, on Android when you sign in, in your RotRun account. RotRun is provided by an independent developer.

If something is not listed here, we do not collect it.

Information we process

On iPhone, and in RotRun’s app extensions via an App Group, RotRun stores settings and activity needed to run the app, including:

  • Apps and categories you choose to block or exclude, Focus and Brick lists, and shield state.
  • Screen Time totals and app names Apple’s Device Activity APIs provide, used for stats, Brain Health, XP, and widgets.
  • Focus session state, streak, Brain Health difficulty, theme, app icon, and intervention-style preferences.
  • Brain state such as daily XP, Ask-my-brain counts, and related scores.
  • Whether you have an active RotRun Premium entitlement (product identifier and renewal date from StoreKit), stored locally.

On iPhone, RotRun does not ask you to register a separate email or password with us for those on-device features.

On Android, RotRun stores settings and activity on the device, including:

  • Apps you choose to block or exclude, Focus and Brick lists, and shield state. When you are signed in, those lists and rules sync to your account.
  • Usage Access totals and app names, used for stats, Brain Health, XP, and widgets. When you are signed in and today’s use is at least a minute, today’s minutes, pickups, top apps, the category Android already assigns to those apps, hourly totals, and daily totals can sync to your account.
  • Focus session state, streak, Brain Health difficulty, theme, app icon, and intervention-style preferences. Streak and focus time can sync when you are signed in.
  • Brain state such as daily XP and Ask-my-brain counts. Daily XP and a display name can sync when you are signed in.
  • The age band you choose in onboarding, such as Under 18 or 18–24. That band stays on the device. It is not a date of birth.
  • Whether you have an active RotRun Premium entitlement from Google Play Billing, stored locally.
  • A name from a RotRun link you open (a bailout, audio slap, or duel), kept on the device for that cameo. A duel name stays until local midnight.

On Android, signing in with Google creates a RotRun account held with Supabase. Google shares an account identifier and email for sign-in. RotRun does not receive your Google password. The sign-in session is stored on the device. Your profile can include your timezone and, if you agree to Rot chat, the time you gave that consent.

Rot chat messages, recent turns, and the context sent with a chat can be stored with that account. The context is local time, timezone, XP, streak, screen-time goal, Brain Health, today’s screen time, pickups, focus time, top apps, whether Premium is active, and the app version. You can ask Rot to remember a fact. You can also ask Rot to forget saved memories. Forgetting clears those memories. Streak and XP stay.

If you start a voice call, the microphone is used for that call. Voice is described below.

Screen Time, Usage Access, and blocking

On iPhone, with your permission, RotRun uses Apple’s Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity (Screen Time) APIs. That lets RotRun show usage, apply shields to apps you select, and refresh blocking.

Apple’s Screen Time authorization may ask you to confirm with Face ID or your device passcode. RotRun does not receive biometric templates or Face ID data.

On iPhone, usage figures and the names of apps you use or try to open are processed on the device and in RotRun’s widget, Device Activity report, and shield extensions. We do not operate a website or server that receives a dashboard of your Screen Time.

On Android, with your permission, RotRun uses Usage Access to show today’s screen time, pickups, and top apps. It uses an Accessibility service to see which app is in front and to show your block screen for apps you chose. That service does not read what you type and does not record the screen. If Accessibility is off, RotRun can use permission to display over other apps as a fallback shield. You can also allow RotRun to ignore battery optimization so blocking keeps running after a restart and when RotRun is swiped away.

Android does not use Face ID for these settings. You turn them on in Android Settings. RotRun does not receive biometric templates.

On Android, the usage figures above are processed on the device and in RotRun’s widgets. When you are signed in, the daily figures described above can sync to your Supabase account. We do not publish a public dashboard of your screen time. RotRun does not read another person’s screen time.

Health and fitness

On iPhone, if you grant Apple Health access, RotRun may:

  • Read walking and running distance, active energy, and workouts for the current day, to award XP and update Brain Clarity.
  • Write a running workout, walking/running distance, and active energy when you log a run or workout in RotRun, so it can appear in Apple Health.

Workouts may be marked as outdoor running in Health metadata. On iPhone, RotRun does not use Core Location and does not request GPS or precise location permission.

On Android, RotRun does not read Health Connect, count sit-ups, log runs, or write workouts. It does not request GPS or precise location permission.

Brain Health is a gamified score based on screen time and related activity. On Android it uses screen time and Focus. It is not a medical or mental-health diagnosis.

Camera

On iPhone, if you grant camera access, RotRun uses the camera:

  • For hands-free sit-up counting (on-device pose detection with Apple’s Vision framework).
  • For FaceTime-style Brain Call interventions that show a live front-camera preview.

On Android, if you grant camera access, RotRun uses the camera only as a Mirror preview: a live front-camera picture during Mirror Mode. It does not count sit-ups, and it does not analyze or store the picture.

On both apps, camera frames used for these features stay on the device for that preview or, on iPhone, for on-device pose detection. RotRun does not record, save, or upload photos or video from the camera.

Notifications, widgets, and Live Activities

On iPhone, RotRun can schedule local notifications (for example Ask-my-brain). These are delivered by iOS on your device. RotRun does not use remote push notifications.

A notification may include the display name of an app you were trying to open, so the intervention can continue.

On iPhone, if you add RotRun widgets or start a Live Activity, they show local data such as Brain Health, rank, XP, and Focus state. That data stays in the App Group on your device.

On Android, RotRun can show local notifications, for example Ask my brain, a block-screen alert, and an upgrade reminder if you turn that on. A notification may include the name of an app you were trying to open. RotRun does not use remote push notifications. Widgets can show local Brain Health, streak, and Focus state.

Leaderboards, Game Center, and Rot chat

On iPhone, leaderboards are optional and use Apple Game Center. If you sign in, Apple may share with RotRun your Game Center display name, player identifier, avatar, and scores. RotRun submits Daily XP to Apple’s Game Center leaderboard and can show other players’ public Game Center names, ranks, and photos.

On iPhone, friend roast tools on the leaderboard use those public names and Daily XP scores only. If you share a receipt, ransom note, audio slap, or duel through the system Share Sheet or iMessage, that share can include a friend’s public Game Center name, their public Daily XP and rank title, and a RotRun link. RotRun does not read another person’s Screen Time and does not keep a friend database on a server. Cameos from those links stay in the App Group on the device that opened the link until local midnight.

Game Center data is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. You can manage Game Center in iOS Settings.

On Android, RotRun does not use Game Center or Google Play Games. When you are signed in, the app submits that day’s XP and a display name to the leaderboard in your Supabase account. The leaderboard can show display names and Daily XP for the current day. This app does not send leaderboard roasts. If you open a RotRun link someone else sent, only the name in that link is kept on your device, as described above.

On Android, Rot chat asks you to sign in with Google before a conversation is sent. Chat text and recent turns are sent to your Supabase account. To generate the reply, the agent-qa function forwards the chat text, recent turns, memories, and the screen-time context (top apps, minutes, and pickups) to xAI (api.x.ai).

Voice on Android is a Premium feature and uses the microphone. Audio from the call is sent to xAI with a short-lived token issued through your RotRun account. The xAI key stays on the server. The app allows up to 5 voice calls a day and 30 a month, and a call stops at 5 minutes, or sooner if the token allows less time. The server can also refuse a call when a daily or monthly voice limit is reached. That limit stays in place until it resets. RotRun does not keep a recording of the call on your account.

Purchases

On iPhone, RotRun Premium is sold as auto-renewable subscriptions through Apple’s App Store (StoreKit). Apple processes payment. RotRun receives entitlement information such as product identifier, whether the subscription is active, and renewal date. We do not receive your full payment card number.

Apple’s privacy policy applies to App Store billing.

On Android, RotRun Premium is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes payment. RotRun receives entitlement information such as product identifier, whether the subscription is active, and expiry or renewal time. We do not receive your full payment card number. When you are signed in, the product id and purchase token can be sent to your Supabase account so that entitlement can be checked.

Google’s privacy policy applies to Play billing.

Advertising ID

RotRun does not use an advertising ID and does not show third-party ads. On Android, the app removes the permission com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, so that advertising ID is not available to RotRun.

How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Provide blocking, Focus, interventions, stats, widgets, and the fitness or Rot chat features you use on that platform.
  • Remember your settings and streak rewards.
  • On Android, when you are signed in, keep your account, block lists, streak, Daily XP, screen-time readings, and Rot chat.
  • Process Premium purchases through Apple on iPhone and through Google Play on Android.
  • Respond if you email us.

We do not use Screen Time, Usage Access, Health, or camera data to serve third-party advertising. We do not currently include third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs in RotRun. Microphone audio is sent to xAI only when you start a voice call on Android.

Sharing

RotRun does not sell your personal information.

On iPhone, information may be processed by Apple when you use Apple services the app relies on: Screen Time / Family Controls, Apple Health, Game Center, App Store / StoreKit, notifications, widgets, and Live Activities.

On iPhone, if you share a leaderboard roast (receipt, ransom, audio slap, or duel) through iMessage or the Share Sheet, you are sending Game Center public names and scores plus an optional RotRun link to people you choose. RotRun does not operate a social backend for those shares.

On Android, information may be processed by Google for Google sign-in and Google Play Billing, and by Supabase for the account, sync, and Rot chat described above. To generate a Rot chat reply, agent-qa forwards the chat text, recent turns, memories, and the screen-time context (top apps, minutes, and pickups) to xAI (api.x.ai). xAI also receives microphone audio when you start a voice call.

If you send Help, Feedback, a bug report, or Contact mail, that message (including any diagnostics you leave in the template, such as app version, iOS or Android version, and device model) goes to rotrunsupport@gmail.com so we can reply. We do not share those emails with advertisers.

Account deletion

Deleting your RotRun account permanently deletes the account, chat history, memories, stats, and leaderboard entry.

On Android, open Settings and choose Delete account, then confirm. The app asks Supabase to delete the account and then signs you out on the device.

Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it separately. On Android, cancel RotRun Premium in Google Play (Manage Subscription in the app, or Google Play’s subscription settings). On iPhone, cancel or manage it in Apple’s subscription settings (Manage Subscription in the app, or Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions).

The same deletion is described for Android 1.0.13+ and the iOS app on Delete your account or data. If you cannot open the app, you can email rotrunsupport@gmail.com from the email you signed in with.

Retention

On iPhone, on-device RotRun data remains until you change settings, delete the app, or reset the device. Uninstalling RotRun removes the app’s local store. Data you wrote to Apple Health, Game Center, or the App Store remains with Apple under Apple’s policies.

On Android, on-device RotRun data remains until you change settings, delete the app, or reset the device. Uninstalling RotRun removes the app’s local store. If Android backup is on for this app, a device backup can include that local data. Data in your RotRun account remains until you delete the account or ask us to delete it. Purchase records remain with Google under Google’s policies.

Emails you send to rotrunsupport@gmail.com are kept as needed to handle your request and for ordinary correspondence.

Your choices and rights

You can:

  • On iPhone, turn off Screen Time, Health, Camera, or Notifications for RotRun in iOS Settings.
  • On iPhone, sign out of Game Center or hide your profile through Apple.
  • On Android, turn off Usage Access, Accessibility, display over other apps, unrestricted battery use, Camera, Microphone, or Notifications for RotRun in Android Settings.
  • On Android, choose Forget everything Rot remembers. Streak and XP stay.
  • Cancel or manage RotRun Premium in Apple’s subscription settings on iPhone, or in Google Play on Android.
  • Delete the app to remove RotRun’s local data.
  • Delete your RotRun account, as described under Account deletion. That does not cancel a Google Play or Apple subscription.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold (for example emails you sent us, and Android account data we hold). Contact rotrunsupport@gmail.com. We cannot retrieve Screen Time or Health samples that exist only on your iPhone or in Apple’s systems, or Usage Access samples that exist only on your Android device. Use the device settings and Apple’s or Google’s tools for those.

Children

RotRun is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. On iPhone, Screen Time features are designed for the person who authorizes Family Controls on the device. On Android, Usage Access and Accessibility are for the person who turns them on for this device. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email rotrunsupport@gmail.com and we will delete it from our inbox where reasonably possible.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when RotRun’s practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change. Continued use after an update means the revised policy applies to that use.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email rotrunsupport@gmail.com.
