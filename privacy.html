Privacy Policy

How RotRun processes information on iPhone and Android.

RotRunLast updated: 6 November 2026Questions? rotrunsupport@gmail.com

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Contact: rotrunsupport@gmail.com

Introduction

RotRun is a digital-wellbeing app for iPhone and Android. This policy covers both apps. Rot chat, voice, screen-time sync, and the leaderboard use the same RotRun account on each.

You sign in with Apple on iPhone and with Google on Android. Both sign-ins go through Supabase.

Couch Reset and Apple Health are iPhone features. They are described in this policy, not in a separate one.

RotRun is provided by an independent developer. If something is not listed here, we do not collect it.

Account and sign-in

Signing in creates a RotRun account held with Supabase. On iPhone, sign-in is Sign in with Apple only. RotRun stores the Supabase user id, the Apple email if Apple shares it, and the sign-in tokens in the Keychain. On Android, sign-in is with Google. Google shares an account identifier and email. RotRun does not receive your Google password, and the sign-in session stays on the device. On both phones, the profile stores the user id and timezone.

The age band you choose in Android onboarding, such as Under 18 or 18–24, stays on the device. It is not a date of birth.

Rot chat and voice

Rot chat and voice go through the Supabase agent-qa function to xAI (api.x.ai). To generate a reply, agent-qa forwards the message, recent turns, memories, and the screen-time report (top apps, minutes, and pickups). Forget memories is in the chat. That clears saved memories and leaves the account in place.

Voice audio is sent to xAI. The xAI key stays on the server. Pro voice is capped. On Android the cap is 5 calls a day, 30 a month, and 5 minutes, or sooner if the token allows less time.

Screen time

Minutes, pickups, and top apps sync to Supabase (screen_time_readings). Daily minutes sync to screen_time_days. On Android, when you are signed in and today’s use is at least a minute, the sync can also include the category Android already assigns to those apps, hourly totals, and daily totals.

RotRun does not publish a public dashboard of your screen time, and it does not read another person’s screen time.

Blocking

On iPhone, blocking uses Apple Screen Time shields (Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity). Screen Time authorization may ask you to confirm with Face ID or your device passcode. RotRun does not receive biometric templates or Face ID data.

On Android, blocking uses Usage Access, an Accessibility service, and display over other apps as a fallback when Accessibility is off. Usage Access provides today’s screen time, pickups, and top apps. Accessibility is used to see which app is in front and to show your block screen. It does not read what you type and does not record the screen. You can allow RotRun to ignore battery optimization so blocking keeps running after a restart and when the app is swiped away. Android block lists and rules sync to the account when you are signed in.

Leaderboard

Leaderboard XP is stored in Supabase (daily_xp). Friends use Game Center only. RotRun does not keep a friend database. The Game Center leaderboard is still on iPhone. Android does not use Game Center.

Couch Reset, camera, and Apple Health

On iPhone, Couch Reset is in the app. The camera counts sit-ups on the device. If you grant Apple Health access, RotRun reads distance, energy, and workouts. It writes nothing to Apple Health.

On Android, the camera is a Mirror preview only. It does not count sit-ups. Those preview frames stay on the device. RotRun does not record, save, or upload photos or video from that preview. Android does not read Health Connect and does not write workouts.

Purchases

RotRun Premium is sold through the App Store on iPhone and Google Play on Android. Apple and Google process payment. RotRun receives entitlement information such as the product identifier and whether the subscription is active. We do not receive your full payment card number. On Android, when you are signed in, the product id and purchase token can be sent to your Supabase account so the entitlement can be checked.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it separately in the App Store or in Google Play.

Advertising and analytics

RotRun does not show ads, does not use an advertising ID, and does not include analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. On Android, the app removes the permission com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, so that advertising ID is not available to RotRun. We do not use screen time, Health, or camera data for third-party advertising.

Notifications

Notifications are local only. RotRun does not use remote push. A notification may include the name of an app you were trying to open, so the intervention can continue. Widgets show local data such as Brain Health and Focus.

How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Provide blocking, Focus, Rot chat, voice, stats, and the leaderboard you use.
  • Keep your account, screen-time readings, Daily XP, and chat when you are signed in.
  • Process Premium purchases through the App Store and Google Play.
  • Reply if you email us.

Sharing

RotRun does not sell your personal information.

Apple processes Sign in with Apple, Screen Time, Game Center, Apple Health, and App Store payments when you use those iPhone services. Google processes Google sign-in and Google Play payments on Android. Supabase holds the account, screen-time sync, Daily XP, and chat. xAI (api.x.ai) receives the message, recent turns, memories, and the screen-time report when Rot replies, and voice audio when you start a call.

If you email rotrunsupport@gmail.com, that message (including diagnostics you leave in, such as app version, iOS or Android version, and device model) is used to reply. We do not share those emails with advertisers.

Deletion

Deleting your RotRun account permanently deletes the account, chat history, memories, stats, and leaderboard entry. Forget memories, in the chat, clears saved memories and does not delete the account.

On Android, open Settings and choose Delete account, then confirm. On iPhone, delete your account at https://ahmedified-47.github.io/rotrun-site/delete-account.html. You can also email rotrunsupport@gmail.com. We complete email deletion requests within 30 days.

Deleting the account does not cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription.

Retention

On-device RotRun data remains until you change settings, delete the app, or reset the device. Uninstalling removes the local store. On Android, a device backup can include that local data if Android backup is on for this app. Data in your Supabase account remains until you delete the account or we complete an email deletion request. Purchase records remain with Apple or Google. RotRun writes nothing to Apple Health. Game Center data remains with Apple.

Your choices

You can:

  • Turn off Screen Time, Health, or Camera for RotRun in iOS Settings, or Usage Access, Accessibility, display over other apps, battery optimization, Camera, or Microphone in Android Settings.
  • Use Forget memories in Rot chat.
  • Cancel or manage RotRun Premium in the App Store or in Google Play.
  • Delete the app to remove local data.
  • Delete your account as described under Deletion. That does not cancel a subscription.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold, including emails you sent us and account data. Contact rotrunsupport@gmail.com. We hold the synced screen-time minutes, pickups, top apps, and daily minutes described above. We cannot retrieve other screen-time detail that exists only on the device, or Health samples that exist only on your iPhone or in Apple’s systems.

Children

RotRun is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Screen Time, Usage Access, and Accessibility are for the person who turns them on for the device. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email rotrunsupport@gmail.com and we will delete it where reasonably possible.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when RotRun’s practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change. Continued use after an update means the revised policy applies to that use.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email rotrunsupport@gmail.com.
